Snake Duel

Each snake starts at 3 cubes. Eat food to grow, reach 6 cubes to win. Shoot the other snake to blow off a cube, a head shot pushes their head back, any other hit closes the gap. Hit a wall, the other snake, or yourself and you lose. Two heads colliding is a tie.

Player 1 - Green
Keyboard: W A S D + Space
Phone: bottom pad
Player 2 - Pink
Keyboard: Arrow keys + Enter
Phone: top pad

On a phone, two players hold opposite ends: Green uses the bottom controls, Pink uses the top controls (they face you).

Play with a friend on another device. Free, peer to peer, no account. You will be Green, your friend Pink.
